This table is the audit trail for [Governance](governance.html) Rules 1–4: for every
foundational resource, where its semantics come from (Xt-EHR), where its FHIR shape comes
from (HL7 Europe Core, or a documented fallback), how it relates to MyHealth@EU's
cross-border services, and exactly what was nationalised and why.
